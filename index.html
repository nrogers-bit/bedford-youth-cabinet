Who we are

An open group for young people who live or go to school in Bedford Borough. Members come from schools across the borough, as well as community groups and other settings.

11–18

Open to all

Any young person aged 11 to 18 can take part.

Up to 25

Extra support

Young people with additional needs or care experience can join up to age 25.

Every

School in the borough

Live or study in Bedford Borough? You’re welcome.

What we do

Members take part in events and projects that give young people a say, and speak up on the issues that matter to them.

Pupil voice events

Helping run conferences where young people share their views.

SEND conferences

Making sure young people with special educational needs and disabilities are heard.

Interview panels

Taking part in interviewing for open roles.

Youth Parliament

Linking up with the UK Youth Parliament and the Member of Youth Parliament for Bedford.

How we’re organised

The group has a youth committee, a bit like a younger version of the government for Bedford.
